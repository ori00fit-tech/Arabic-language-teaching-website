Moroccan Darija

The everyday spoken Arabic of Morocco. Perfect for travel, family connections, living in Morocco or simply enjoying Moroccan culture.

  • Real conversations from day one

FAQ

Common questions

How are the lessons delivered?

All lessons are live and online through a video platform such as Zoom or Google Meet. You'll receive the link after registration.

How big are the groups?

Groups are kept small so every student gets time to speak and ask questions.

Should I learn MSA or Darija?

Choose MSA for reading, formal Arabic and use across the Arab world. Choose Darija if you want to speak with people in Morocco. Many students start with one and add the other later.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I join?

Absolutely. Beginner groups start from the alphabet and basic phrases — no previous knowledge needed.

What about pricing?

You can subscribe monthly (best value) or pay per lesson. See the pricing section for current rates. The placement chat is always free.
